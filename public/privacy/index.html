What this local prototype does

These files provide text, local navigation and local styling. They contain no forms, analytics scripts, cookies set by application code, phone tracking or lead delivery. No personal details can be submitted through a page. These are statements about the prototype code, not a promise about a future hosting provider.

Hosting is not configured

No Netlify site or GitHub repository is selected for this draft. Hosting access logs, platform processing and retention have not been evaluated. A future production notice must describe the actual services and data flow rather than assuming that a static page involves no processing.

What must be decided before requests exist

Local Loop is the operator reported by Atlas, run by Ryan Horne. The proposed future recipient is North Star Heating and Air Conditioning Inc. (Dagsboro, DE), under the Seaford arrangement reported by Atlas; no information is sent to it by this local prototype. Before activation, document the information requested, purpose, recipient terms, processors, access permissions, retention periods and deletion process. Establish the operator’s approved privacy contact and applicable rights process. No contact address, retention period or legal basis is invented here.

Analytics, calls and texting remain off

If analytics, call handling, recording or SMS are proposed later, review necessity, providers, consent and disclosures before enabling them. Do not send personal information through analytics events or URL parameters. No such features are active in this draft.

This is not a final privacy notice

Legal review and implementation checks are required. The final notice must match the deployed system, confirm the reported Local Loop operator identity and include an approved contact. Until then there are no referral requests, and no privacy request form is presented as operational.
