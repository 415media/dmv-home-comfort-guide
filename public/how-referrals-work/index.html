A named recipient must come first

The proposed receiving contractor is North Star Heating and Air Conditioning Inc. (Dagsboro, DE), as reported by Atlas. Before a future request feature can launch, the reported agreement, service area, offered services, permissions and response expectations must be confirmed for publication. The reader must see the recipient and understand that submitting a request does not itself book service.

Disclosure and choice

Any paid placement, referral compensation or free pilot arrangement must be disclosed. The proposed pilot would send a request to one named recipient, not silently distribute it to a list. That is a proposal, not an operating promise; recipient rules and informed consent still need approval.

Permission before contact

Legal-reviewed consent must explain what information would be sent and who may call or email about the request. Any texting or recording would need its own appropriate review and disclosures. No consent checkbox, SMS capability or recording exists here.

Qualification, disputes and accountability

Before activation, agree what counts as a service inquiry, how duplicates and disputes are handled, what response expectations mean and where complaints go. No current response time or booking outcome is promised. These operating rules and the privacy data flow must be approved together.
